# Blogdel rules

These standards apply to new and regenerated articles and to changes to article publication behavior. Detailed implementation notes live in [documentation/article-quality-standard.md](documentation/article-quality-standard.md); thumbnail and operating rules are in [documentation/rules.md](documentation/rules.md).

## Article length and structure

- Minimum **650 words** for a standard article.
- Minimum **three useful `##` sections**.
- Do not add filler to reach the minimum. Each section must contribute supported explanation or a useful answer.

## Evidence and references

- Supply either a context summary of at least 120 characters or at least three meaningful facts tied to supplied source references.
- Include at least two distinct HTTPS source references.
- Preserve the supplied reference set in the article; do not invent URLs.
- Check the quality and accuracy of sources and claims during editorial review. Automated checks cannot verify truth or usefulness by themselves.

## Topic overlap and publishing

- Compare each new title with the 30 most recent published titles in its category.
- Treat normalized token-set Jaccard similarity of 0.72 or higher as recent title overlap.
- Keep any article that fails a quality check in review. Do not lower or bypass a threshold without an explicit standards change.

These are Blogdel editorial minimums; they are not Google or AdSense word-count requirements.
